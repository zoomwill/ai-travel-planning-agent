# Travel photography knowledge

## Planning a photography day

Choose a small number of locations with different visual character: a quiet morning street, an
indoor or shaded midday subject, and an evening viewpoint. Build transfer time and equipment breaks
into the itinerary. Weather changes can be useful: overcast light softens portraits and details,
while rain can create reflections, but outdoor plans still need a safe indoor alternative.

## Street photography etiquette

Stay aware of local rules, private property, and people's comfort. Do not block pavements or shop
entrances. Ask permission when a close portrait could make someone identifiable or uncomfortable.
Travel light in crowded areas and keep valuable equipment secure.

## Visual variety

Neighborhood markets, gardens, transit scenes, architecture, and elevated views tell different
parts of a destination's story. Repeating the same famous viewpoint rarely adds as much value as
spending time observing one neighborhood carefully.
